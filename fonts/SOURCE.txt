NotoSansCuneiform-Inscription.ttf contains the space and all 26 unique
cuneiform characters used by arkInstructionCuneiform in shrine.js.
It is loaded in p5 preload() and drawn as glyph paths, so the inscription
does not depend on fonts installed on the visitor's device.

Regenerate from the upstream file with FontTools 4.68.0:
pyftsubset NotoSansCuneiform-Regular.ttf --unicodes=U+0020,U+12000,U+12040,U+12049,U+1204D,U+1207A,U+1208A,U+1208D,U+12157,U+121AA,U+121B3,U+121F7,U+121FB,U+12220,U+1223E,U+12246,U+1227A,U+1227F,U+12291,U+12292,U+122AD,U+122BA,U+122D7,U+122FE,U+12305,U+12311,U+12363 --output-file=NotoSansCuneiform-Inscription.ttf

If the inscription changes, include its new characters in this subset.
